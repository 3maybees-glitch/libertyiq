## How to use

1. Memorize the **acronym** (gold banner).
2. Open with the **Opening frame**.
3. Walk the lettered **path** top → bottom (follow the ↓ arrows).
4. When interrupted, jump to the matching **They say → You say** counter.
5. Finish on the **Close →** line.

## Acronym cheatsheet

- **ALIVE** (Pro-Life): At fertilization · Life = person · Inalienable right · Vulnerable · Expansion risk
- **BOLTS** (Illegal Immigration): Borders & law · Obligation to citizens · Legal immigrants first · Threats stopped · Shared culture
- **ARMED** (Second Amendment): Authority to defend · Right in Constitution · Millions of defensive uses · Evil of disarmament · Disarmament fails
- **CREED** (Marriage: One Man & One Woman): Creation design · Reason / natural law · Elevate children · Expression liberty · Definition matters
- **FACTS** (Two Biological Sexes): Formed male/female · Anatomy immutable · Children protected · Truth is love · Speech/conscience free
- **CLAIM** (Pro-Israel): Covenant · Legal/historical claim · Alliance · Integrity (moral democracy) · Mapped in prophecy
- **GUARD** (Strong National Security): God-ordained mandate · Undermine aggression · Advance civilization · Ready forces · Doctrine war
- **SENSE** (Anti-Climate Alarmism): Stewardship · Examine claims · Neighbor flourishing · Solid energy · Expose narrative
- **LIMIT** (Limited Government): Lesson of kings · Industry & property · Maximize liberty · Incentives win · Thrift over debt
- **LIGHT** (Anti-Critical Race Theory): Likeness of God · Illogic of CRT · Genuine progress · Harm to kids · True Gospel unity
- **SERVE** (Crime & Justice): Sword mandate · Enforce safety · Rule of law · Vet threats · Elevate victims
- **FORCE** (Role of the Military): Fight/defend mandate · Ordered civilian control · Readiness/lethality · Character/ethos · Ends limited (just war)
- **CURES** (Against Universal Government Healthcare): Choice · Unleash markets · Rationing honesty · Expand innovation · Steward the vulnerable
- **HUMAN** (AI Governance): Human dignity · Unlock innovation · Moderate speech risks · Accountable narrow rules · Nation leads vs rivals
