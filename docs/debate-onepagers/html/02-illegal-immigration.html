Illegal Immigration

One-page talking points
Issue 2 of 11
libertyiq.org
Opening frame → start here

Compassion and the rule of law are not enemies. Ordered, legal immigration strengthens a nation; lawlessness harms citizens and migrants alike.

Debate path

1

Rule of law: God-ordained government requires enforceable borders and laws (Rom 13; Acts 17:26).

↓
2

Stewardship: Nations must prioritize citizens’ welfare, capacity, and security.

↓
3

Justice: Skipping the line punishes those who wait and sacrifice to come legally.

↓
4

Security: Open borders empower cartels, trafficking, and unvetted threats.

↓
5

Assimilation: Manageable legal flows allow newcomers to join one people under shared laws.

Counters

They say

“Jesus was a refugee”

→
You say

His family fled to Egypt under the laws of that time and returned when safe — not a case for ignoring immigration law.

They say

“We’re all immigrants”

→
You say

Most ancestors came under the laws then in force. Lawful heritage doesn’t erase today’s need for borders.

They say

“They do jobs Americans won’t”

→
You say

Use legal guest-worker paths. Labor needs don’t require nullifying sovereignty.

They say

“Enforcement is cruel”

→
You say

Cruelty is chaos: trafficking, wage suppression, and deaths at the border. Clear laws + dignity in enforcement.

Close → ⟶

Frame: generous legal immigration + real enforcement. Love and law together.
