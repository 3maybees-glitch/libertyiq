Counters

They say

“Government provides necessary services”

→
You say

Yes — core ones: defense, courts, policing (Rom 13). Most else is better private or local.

They say

“Without regulation, exploitation”

→
You say

Fraud and force are already illegal. Competition and reputation discipline better than endless rules.

They say

“We need safety nets”

→
You say

Charity and family first. Government monopolies often trap poverty; civil society humanizes help.

They say

“The rich don’t pay their share”

→
You say

The top earners already fund most income taxes. “Fair share” often means “higher share.”
