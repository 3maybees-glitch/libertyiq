Opening frame → start here

Marriage is not a private lifestyle label — it is a pre-political institution oriented to man, woman, and children.

CREED path

C
Creation design

Male and female; “one flesh” — affirmed by Jesus (Gen 1–2; Matt 19).

↓
R
Reason / natural law

Bodies are complementary; only male–female union is procreative by nature.

↓
E
Elevate children

Kids do best with a mother and a father; fatherlessness is a social crisis.

↓
E
Expression liberty

Redefinition has coerced bakers, florists, and adoption agencies.

↓
D
Definition matters

If marriage means anything, it cannot mean everything.

Counters

They say

“Jesus never mentioned homosexuality”

→

He reaffirmed Genesis male–female marriage (Matt 19 / Mark 10). That settles the design.

They say

“Love is love”

→
You say

Love is necessary, not sufficient. The institution exists for a purpose beyond adult feelings.

They say

“Same-sex couples can be good parents”

→
You say

Individual cases ≠ the normative ideal. Policy should favor what is best for children as a class.

They say

“This is just Old Testament law”

→
You say

Jesus and the New Testament reaffirm the creation pattern — not a temporary civil code.
