Second Amendment

One-page talking points
Issue 3 of 11
libertyiq.org
Opening frame → start here

The right to keep and bear arms is the right to defend innocent life — against criminals today and tyranny tomorrow.

Debate path

1

Self-defense: Scripture treats defending family and home as legitimate (Ex 22; Luke 22:36).

↓
2

Constitution: “The right of the people… shall not be infringed” — Heller affirms an individual right.

↓
3

Practical: Defensive gun uses vastly outnumber criminal uses; police can’t be everywhere.

↓
4

History: Major 20th-century democides followed citizen disarmament.

↓
5

Logic: Criminals ignore gun laws. Control disarms the law-abiding first.

Counters

They say

“Turn the other cheek”

→
You say

Jesus addressed personal insult, not abandoning the innocent to violence.

They say

“Only the military needs guns”

→
You say

The Founders wrote the 2A to secure the people’s right — not a government monopoly on force.

They say

“Other countries have fewer shootings”

→
You say

Culture, family breakdown, and mental health drive violence. High-ownership Switzerland/Israel aren’t US-style chaos.

They say

“We just want common-sense laws”

→
You say

Define them. Many “common sense” proposals fail criminals while burdening the innocent. Target crime, not rights.

Close → ⟶

Reject the false choice: liberty or safety. Armed citizens are part of safety.
